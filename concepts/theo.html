    AUTHEO / DEVELOPER DOCUMENTATION

    THEO

    THEO is the token Autheo uses to price Marketplace resources (compute, storage, bandwidth, hosting). This page covers only what developers can read today; it is not investment information.

    Display price feed

    dev.autheo.com publishes a reference price at https://dev.autheo.com/api/market-reference/theo. On 2026-10-12 it returned JSON with these fields:

    Field Meaning
    available Whether a price could be fetched
    price_usd Reference price in USD
    change_24h_pct 24-hour change, percent
    liquidity_usd Reported liquidity of the source pool
    source, source_url Where the price came from (that day: Dexscreener, Base / Hydrex pool)
    fetched_at_ms Retrieval time (not proof of a recent trade)
    stale True when the value is too old to use
    display_only Always true: for display, not for settlement or quotes

    The Autheo MCP server reads this feed (or an operator-supplied feed) and refuses to convert amounts when the price is stale or unavailable. Marketplace amounts stay exact decimal strings.

    Where THEO trades

    autheo-os/theo-atlas (commit 0294acd) is a local read-only market monitor. It covers MEXC THEO/USDT, the Hydrex THEO/USDC pool on Base, and Uniswap v3 THEO/USDG and THEO/NVDA pools on Robinhood Chain. Robinhood Chain pools are not a claim that the Robinhood brokerage lists THEO. It deliberately omits market cap and cross-chain FDV, and treats pool reserves as reported liquidity, not a guaranteed sell quote.

    Settlement

    DevHub source contains THEO settlement verification: plan checkout (exact ERC-20 transfer to a treasury) and Marketplace settlement through the MarketplaceAtomicFeeSplit V2 contract on the Autheo testnet (chain 785). DevHub verifies payments; it never sends or signs them. Mainnet settlement is planned. See THEO economy.
